Our peace missionaries are equipped with mediation skills, cultural sensitivity training, and a deep commitment to gospel-centered reconciliation. They serve as bridges between conflicting parties, facilitating dialogue, healing, and sustainable peace agreements in communities across Ethiopia.

    Community Mediation
    Facilitating dialogue between conflicting tribal and cultural groups.
  • 📖
    Gospel-Centered Reconciliation
    Rooting peace work in biblical principles of forgiveness and restoration.
  • 🤝
    Sustainable Peace Agreements
    Building long-term frameworks for community coexistence and mutual respect.

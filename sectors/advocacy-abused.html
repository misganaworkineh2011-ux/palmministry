Advocacy for the Abused in Religious Institutions

በሃይማኖት ተቋማት ውስጥ ለተበደሉ ወገኖች ጥበቃና ድምፅ መሆን

A highly confidential, professional, and compassionate system for individuals recovering from administrative oversight, spiritual trauma, or institutional abuse.

Strategic Framework

  • 1

    Policy Advocacy

    Partnering with legal experts, regulatory bodies, and the Inter-Religious Council of Ethiopian Evangelical Churches to improve safety policies.

  • 2

    Trauma-Informed Rehabilitation

    Providing a safe environment for psychological and emotional recovery with trained counselors and pastoral care.

  • 3

    Breaking the "Shame Culture"

    Fostering honest institutional dialogue over harmful silence that enables continued abuse.

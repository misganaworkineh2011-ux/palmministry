Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Our Commitment

PALM Ministry (palmministry.org) is committed to protecting your privacy, especially for individuals using our confidential advocacy channels.

Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide through membership applications, donation forms, contact forms, volunteer registrations, and confidential support requests.

Confidential Advocacy Data

Messages submitted through our secure advocacy portal are encrypted and accessed only by designated executive counseling leadership. This data is never shared publicly or used for marketing.

Donations

Payment information is processed through secure third-party gateways (Chapa, Telebirr, Stripe, PayPal). PALM does not store full payment card details.

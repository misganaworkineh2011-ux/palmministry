Our Identity & Mandate

Background (Article 1)

Peace Advocacy Leadership Ministry (PALM) was established in response to the urgent need for structural and cultural transformation in Ethiopian society. We recognize that deep-rooted societal friction, systemic injustice, and institutional abuse — particularly within religious contexts — have created lasting wounds that require both spiritual renewal and practical advocacy. PALM exists to bridge these gaps through gospel-centered peace, transformational leadership, and compassionate action.

Vision & Mission (Article 6)

Our vision is to see a society where structural and cultural justice prevails, free from institutional abuses, where every individual can flourish in dignity and peace. Our mission is to equip leaders, advocate for the abused, deploy peace missionaries, care for creation, and engage youth — all grounded in sound Christian doctrine and servant-hearted leadership.

Article 5

Statement of Faith

የእምነት አቋም

PALM fully adheres to orthodox, sound Christian doctrine as articulated in Article 5 of our constitution. We affirm the absolute authority and inerrancy of Scripture, the Triune Godhead — Father, Son, and Holy Spirit — the deity and humanity of Jesus Christ, His atoning death and bodily resurrection, salvation by grace through faith alone, and the call to live lives of holiness, justice, and compassion in accordance with biblical teaching.

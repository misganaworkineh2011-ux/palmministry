In alignment with Article 9 of our constitution, PALM is actively laying the groundwork to formally establish The Palm College — an accredited higher education institution dedicated to peace and leadership studies.

Why The Palm College?

Ethiopia needs leaders trained not only in theology and administration, but in peace advocacy, conflict resolution, institutional governance, and trauma-informed pastoral care. The Palm College will offer advanced certifications that meet this urgent need.

Timeline & Vision

We aim to launch within the next two years, offering programs in:

  • Peace Advocacy & Structural Justice
  • Transformational Leadership & Governance
  • Trauma-Informed Pastoral Care
  • Environmental Peacebuilding & Creation Care

The Palm College represents PALM's long-term commitment to institutional longevity and educational excellence — building a generation of leaders equipped for lasting transformation.

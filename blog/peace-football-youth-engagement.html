On any given Saturday in Addis Ababa, you'll find the PALM Peace Football Team on the pitch — youth in olive green and gold jerseys, learning far more than football skills.

Sport as a Bridge

Football transcends tribal, cultural, and economic boundaries. When young people train together, compete together, and celebrate together, structural isolation begins to break down.

  • Leadership values instilled through team captaincy and mentorship
  • Discipline and character development through regular training
  • Community building through local peace matches
  • Countering youth isolation with belonging and purpose

Player tryouts are open throughout the year. Join us in using the universal language of sport to build peace.

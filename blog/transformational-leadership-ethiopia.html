Transformational Leadership: Equipping Pastors for Structural Justice

Across Addis Ababa and beyond, PALM's Peace Advocacy and Transformational Leadership Training program is reshaping how religious leaders understand their role — from passive administrators to active advocates for justice and peace.

Beyond Institutional Management

Many leadership programs focus on organizational efficiency. PALM's curriculum goes deeper, addressing the ethical, legal, and advocacy capacities that leaders need to confront structural violence and institutional dysfunction.

Three Core Modules

  • Foundations of Peace Advocacy — Identifying structural violence and speaking up safely
  • Transformational Leadership — Shifting from abusive power to servant-hearted governance
  • Advanced Mediation — Practical tools for institutional and community disputes

Over 500 leaders have completed PALM training workshops. The impact ripples outward as equipped pastors, elders, and youth influencers bring gospel-centered peace into their communities.

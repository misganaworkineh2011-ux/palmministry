Faith & the Green Legacy: Tree Planting as Peacebuilding

Faith & Green Legacy፡ Tree Planting እንደ Peacebuilding

📅 April 10, 2026 ✍️ PALM Creation Care Team ⏱ 5 min read

Environmental degradation is not merely an ecological crisis — in Ethiopia, it is often a source of community conflict. Scarce water, deforested land, and resource competition fuel friction between neighbors and tribes.

Stewardship as Peace Work

PALM's Creation Care sector connects biblical stewardship with practical environmental action. Through large-scale tree planting campaigns aligned with Ethiopia's National Green Legacy initiative, we mobilize faith communities to heal the land together.

When communities plant trees side by side, they build trust alongside ecosystems.

Our Green Day events welcome volunteers of all ages. You can also sponsor seedlings or tool kits to support rural communities participating in environmental peacebuilding.
